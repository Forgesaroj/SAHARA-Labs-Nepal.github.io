COMPANY / WHY WE EXIST

Built beside real business work.

SAHARA LABS is a Nepal-based product studio led by Saroj Rijal. Our product decisions begin with real voucher entry, stock movement, reconciliation, approvals, service flow, and owner reporting.

That proximity keeps the work practical: professional speed without weak controls, and modern architecture without erasing the workflows experienced operators already understand.
