ABOUT SAHARA LABS

Built beside real businesses, not just demo data.

SAHARA LABS is a Nepal-based product studio led by Saroj Rijal. We build software around the daily realities of retail, wholesale, manufacturing, accounting, and hospitality.

That proximity matters. Product decisions begin with real voucher entry, stock movement, reconciliation, approvals, service flow, and owner reporting—not a generic dashboard template.

OUR MISSION

Make serious business software feel fast, clear, and trustworthy.

We combine proven accounting discipline with modern web architecture, while protecting the dense workflows experienced operators depend on.

BASE Nepal

Local workflow knowledge with a globally extensible architecture.

FLAGSHIP SAHARA CORE

Accounting, inventory, reporting, and business operations.

METHOD Evidence-led

Decisions, implementation, verification, and release remain distinct.

HOW WE BUILD

Correctness first. Then control, speed, and scale.

Business software earns trust through repeatable results. Our engineering principles are designed to protect that trust as the product grows.

01

Accounting discipline

Financial and inventory effects must remain balanced, atomic, traceable, and reconcilable.

02

Auditability by design

Important actions should carry identity, reason, time, and an explainable history.

03

Professional speed

Keyboard efficiency, predictable focus, compact reports, and fast entry are product requirements.

04

Secure boundaries

Permissions, company isolation, and server-side validation belong at trusted boundaries.

05

Evidence over claims

Runtime behavior and reproducible verification determine status—not screenshots or optimistic labels.

06

Practical adaptability

Nepal-first workflow knowledge informs the product without restricting its long-term reach.

TECHNOLOGY

Modern foundations for long-lived business software.

SAHARA CORE is designed as a modular TypeScript platform with a PostgreSQL accounting foundation, clear service boundaries, and deployment options for cloud and locally managed environments.

Architecture intent Small complete modules, trusted domain rules, explicit transactions, and measurable verification.
Application TypeScript · Bun · Elysia
Web React · Vite · TanStack Router
Data PostgreSQL 17 · Drizzle ORM
Identity Better Auth · RBAC · Row-level isolation
Operations pg-boss · Pino · React PDF
